Four answers, never a bluff

It knows the difference between wrong, and don't-know.

Entailed

The record directly supports it — with the source and the dates it held.

Contradicted

The record directly says the opposite — a withdrawal, a supersession.

Unknown

No basis in the documents you gave it. It refuses to invent one.

Derived

An inference — shown with its rule and every parent fact, never as a stored fact.

Honest about what it is.

What it does

  • Answers from verified, source-backed facts
  • Knows when a fact was true (validity intervals, supersession)
  • Exports a proof pack you can verify offline and hand to an auditor
  • Says Unknown instead of guessing
  • Runs locally — data never leaves your machine

What it is not

  • Not a chatbot and not "an AGI"
  • Not automatic PDF understanding — facts are reviewed by a person
  • Not a source of autonomous decisions
  • Not legal or compliance advice
  • Hashes prove the export wasn't edited — not who issued the document
